Collaborators

My research is carried out in collaboration with a number of colleagues across several institutions. At FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg I work with Enrique Zuazua and, on machine-learning topics, with Antonio Álvarez-López, as well as with Alexander Keimer (Universität Rostock) and Hossein Nick Zinat Matin (École Polytechnique) on nonlocal conservation laws. My work on partial differential equations is developed together with Vittorino Pata and Filippo Dell'Oro (Politecnico di Milano), Timothée Crin-Barat (FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg, now Université de Toulouse), and Ramón Quintanilla (Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya). On spectral stability I collaborate with Veronica Felli and Roberto Ognibene (Milano-Bicocca), and on hybrid modelling of brain dynamics with Daniel Lopez-Montero (FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg) and Xenia Kobeleva (Ruhr-Universität Bochum).
